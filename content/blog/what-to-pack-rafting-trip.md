You will get wet — that's the whole point. Here's how to pack so you're comfortable before, during, and after your rafting trip.

## Wear

- Quick-dry shorts or leggings (avoid cotton — it stays wet and heavy)
- A t-shirt or rash guard, ideally quick-dry
- Secured footwear — sports sandals or old sneakers with a back strap. No flip-flops; they'll come off in the water.
- Sunscreen, applied before you arrive

## Bring in a Sealed Bag

- A full change of clothes for after the trip
- A towel
- A dry bag or ziplock for your phone/wallet if you want to keep them nearby (though we recommend leaving valuables at your hotel)
- Any personal medication you may need

## Leave at Your Hotel

- Jewelry and watches
- Non-waterproof electronics you're not willing to risk
- Loose sunglasses without a strap (they will end up in the river)
- Anything you'd be upset to lose in the water

## On the Day

Arrive a little before your reporting time — every trip starts with a safety briefing, plus life jacket and helmet fitting, so build in a few extra minutes. If you're booking our [Camping + Rafting Combo](/packages/camping-rafting-combo), pack an extra layer for the evening; it gets cool by the river after sunset.
