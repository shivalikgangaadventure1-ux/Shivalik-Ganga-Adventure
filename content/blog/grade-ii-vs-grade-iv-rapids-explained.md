Every rafting package lists a "grade" — but what does Grade II or Grade IV actually mean for your trip? Rapid grades follow the International Scale of River Difficulty, running from Grade I (easiest) to Grade VI (essentially unraftable commercially).

## Grade I–II: Easy to Moderate

Small waves, a few riffles, and mostly clear passages. This is where our [Brahmpuri to Rishikesh package](/packages/brahmpuri-to-rishikesh) sits — a gentle, forgiving introduction that's ideal for first-timers, families, and children above the minimum age.

## Grade III: Moderate to Difficult

Irregular waves, some larger drops, and rapids that require active paddling and teamwork to navigate cleanly. Most of our core routes — including [Shivpuri to Rishikesh](/packages/shivpuri-to-rishikesh) and [Marine Drive to Rishikesh](/packages/marine-drive-to-rishikesh) — fall in this range. Expect to get properly soaked.

## Grade III+ to IV: Difficult to Very Difficult

Powerful, continuous rapids with larger drops and less margin for error. Routes like [Kaudiyala to Rishikesh](/packages/kaudiyala-to-rishikesh) and our most intense run, [Kaudiyala to Shivpuri Extreme](/packages/kaudiyala-to-shivpuri-extreme), sit here — best suited to rafters with some prior experience or strong water confidence.

## So Which Grade Should You Book?

- **First time rafting, traveling with kids, or want a relaxed float?** Start at Grade II.
- **Want a real adventure without going to the extreme?** Grade III is the sweet spot most rafters pick.
- **Experienced, fit, and chasing the biggest rapids on the Ganga?** Grade III+ to IV routes are built for you.
