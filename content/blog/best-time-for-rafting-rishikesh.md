If you're planning a rafting trip on the Ganga, timing matters almost as much as which stretch you choose. Here's what to expect through the year.

## September to November: Post-Monsoon Peak

Right after the monsoon retreats, the Ganga is running full and fast. Rapids are at their most powerful, water levels are high, and the river has a strong, exciting current. This is peak season for experienced rafters and thrill-seekers who want the biggest water of the year.

## December to February: Winter Rafting

Water levels drop and the river calms down, but rafting continues year-round in Rishikesh. Mornings and evenings get cold, so warm layers under your life jacket are worth packing. This is a quieter season with smaller crowds at the base points.

## March to May: Spring Season

As temperatures rise, this is one of the most comfortable windows to raft — pleasant weather, moderate water levels, and a good balance of rapids and calm floating stretches. A popular choice for families and first-timers.

## June to Mid-July: Monsoon Closure

Rafting operations pause during the peak monsoon (roughly mid-June through July) when the Ganga's water level and current become unsafe for commercial rafting. Exact dates shift year to year based on rainfall and official river safety guidance — always check with us before planning a monsoon-season trip.

If you want the biggest rapids, aim for September–November. If you want a calmer, more relaxed introduction to rafting, March–May or the winter months work well.
